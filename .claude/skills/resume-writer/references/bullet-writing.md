# Bullet writing

## Shape
- One idea per bullet. Aim for one line, at most two (about 15 to 28 words).
- Pattern: strong verb + what was done + how (tool or method) + result.
- Past tense for finished work, present tense for the current role.
- No "I", "my", "we". No filler adjectives (hard-working, passionate, innovative, various, multiple). No "responsible for", "worked on", "helped with" when a specific verb is true.

## Results
- Use a number only if it is in the profile entry you cite.
- No number? Show scope instead: what kind of system, how many items, who used it, which tools. Do not invent a percentage.
- Do not turn a count into a rate or a rate into a count.

## Wording for the job
- If the job posting says "vulnerability reports" and the profile bullet says "wrote findings for developers", you may use "vulnerability reports" only if it is the same thing. If unsure, ask the user.
- Spell out an acronym once with the short form, for example "Applicant Tracking System (ATS)", if both forms appear in the posting.

## Ordering inside an entry
- Most relevant to the target job first, then the strongest result.
- Two to four bullets for the strongest entries, one or two for the rest. Cut anything that does not help this job.

## Verbs (plain, accurate, choose the true one)
- Built, wrote, designed, tested, found, reported, fixed, reviewed, automated, configured, analysed, taught, led, organised, reduced, increased, delivered, maintained, documented.
- Use "led" only if the profile says the person led.
- Use "reduced" or "increased" only with a profile fact that shows a change.
